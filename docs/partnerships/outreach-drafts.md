## Outreach rules

- Send a small number of relevant, individually reviewed messages.
- Use an organization-owned sender and public organizational contact route.
- Do not add private personal details, scrape individuals or infer relationships.
- Do not call a prospect a partner, imply endorsement or attach its logo.
- Do not promise clinical outcomes, individual-level data, exclusivity, paid
  services, infrastructure or legal terms.
- Stop after one follow-up unless the organization responds or explicitly invites
  further contact.
- Do not send until the relevant product surface passes the pilot release gate.

## Discovery call agenda — 25 minutes

2. Real product demo plus current limitations — 6 min
3. One pilot learning objective — 4 min
4. Participant privacy, safety and support boundaries — 4 min
5. Cohort size, timing, success metric and stop conditions — 4 min
6. Decision: no fit, revise, or prepare a written pilot scope — 3 min
