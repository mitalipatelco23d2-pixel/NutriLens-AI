# India partnership prospect research notes

Status: `READY_PARTNERSHIP_OUTREACH` as research only. No outreach was sent.

## Scope and method

The companion CSV contains 31 India-friendly organizations across campuses,
dietetic/nutrition groups, fitness/wellness platforms, health-tech communities,
startup ecosystems and packaged-food brands. Each route came from a public
organizational website available on 2026-09-02. No private address, scraped
social profile, inferred personal email or purchased list was used.

Files:

- `prospect-tracker-template.csv` — reusable empty tracker schema;
- `india-public-prospects.csv` — researched, not-contacted candidates.

The source URL in each CSV row is the citation for the public contact route.
Public contact information can change; re-open that source immediately before
any message is sent.

## Relationship tiers

- **WARM: 0.** No real personal or institutional relationship can be inferred
  from repository or public-web evidence. The owner should add warm prospects
  only after confirming a genuine relationship and the permitted introduction
  path.
- **MEDIUM: 21.** A clear small-pilot fit and/or explicit public organizational
  route, often local, chapter-level or partnership-oriented.
- **COLD: 10.** Larger, higher-governance, enterprise, government, national-body
  or potentially overlapping product organizations.

Tier means outreach practicality, not endorsement, product quality or likelihood
of acceptance.

## Pre-send checklist

1. Confirm the relevant NutriLens release surface is ready for the proposed
   pilot; a draft marketing pack is not a release decision.
2. Re-open the row's official source and verify the organization, route and
   purpose.
3. Confirm the sender is an approved Yash AI Technologies account with a public
   signature and reply address.
4. Tailor one approved outreach draft to one organization; do not bulk merge.
5. Record the message date, sender, lawful/relationship basis and next action in
   a working copy of the tracker.
6. Send at most one follow-up, then close `NO_RESPONSE` unless invited to
   continue.
7. Mark `REPLIED`, `MEETING`, `NOT_A_FIT`, `PAUSED_RELEASE_GATE` or
   `DO_NOT_CONTACT` precisely. Never mark `PARTNER` from a positive reply alone.
