## One-page overview

### A practical food-label literacy pilot

NutriLens X helps people turn a packaged-food label into a clearer view of the
package's serving, nutrition, ingredient and explicit allergen evidence. It is a
consumer general-wellness product from Yash AI Technologies—not a medical
device, clinical decision system or replacement for the physical label.

### What a partner can test

- whether participants complete their first useful label scan;
- whether serving, nutrient and ingredient information becomes easier to find;
- whether participants take a deliberate next action, such as saving a result
  or adding an actual consumption record on a verified supported surface;
- which label layouts remain confusing or require enhanced processing;
- whether an evidence-first workshop improves food-label literacy.

### Initial low-cost pilot format

- 2–4 weeks, opt-in and non-clinical;
- 20–100 invited adult participants, adjusted to verified capacity;
- one 30-minute introduction or recorded demo;
- a small set of ordinary packaged-food label tasks;
- optional anonymous feedback survey managed under an approved notice;
- weekly operational check-in and end-of-pilot summary;
- no paid commitment in the discovery phase.

### What NutriLens X does not offer

- diagnosis, treatment, prescriptions or regulated medical services;
- a guarantee that extraction is complete or that a product is safe;
- sponsored changes to nutrition interpretation;
- access to identifiable participant nutrition records;
- Health Connect data in partner exports;
- research analytics before legal/privacy approval;
- a contract, exclusivity, paid media or SLA in an exploratory email.

### Product truth at drafting time

The authenticated production system has evidence for durable owner-scoped scan
jobs and a structured real-package result containing nutrition, ingredient and
explicit milk-allergen evidence. Enhanced server processing can be slower. A
signed Play release, complete physical release-device gate and every proposed
consumer action are not yet represented as complete by this pack. A pilot starts
only after its required release surfaces are marked ready.

### Independence and privacy principles

NutriLens interpretation remains independent. A sponsor cannot pay to alter the
meaning of package evidence. Partners receive no identifiable personal
nutrition or scan data. Any aggregate pilot report must use minimum group sizes,
suppression rules, a documented purpose and an approved privacy/legal basis.
Health Connect data is excluded. NutriLens X does not sell identifiable user
health or nutrition data.

### Good first partners

Campus wellness/student groups, fitness communities, registered dietetic
professional communities, food-label literacy initiatives and transparent food
brands willing to support independent evidence are the best early fits.

### Proposed next step

A 25-minute discovery call to select one participant group, one measurable
learning objective and one no-cost or bounded-cost pilot. No commitment is made
until scope, privacy, responsibilities and success measures are approved in
writing.

## Partner privacy summary

- Participants use their own authenticated accounts.
- A partner does not receive account-level scans, diary records or goals.
- Raw scan images are processing inputs and are not retained after processing
  under the current documented processing model; structured private results may
  remain until the user deletes them or the applicable retention rule acts.
- A participant can decline the pilot without losing ordinary product access.
- Any survey must state who operates it, what it collects and how long responses
  are retained.
- Aggregate reporting is a separate, legally reviewed scope. It is not enabled
  merely by agreeing to a product pilot.
- No Health Connect data, payment data or direct identifiers enter a partner
  report.

The public privacy policy and final pilot notice control over this summary.

## Partnership FAQ

### Is NutriLens X a nutritionist or medical tool?

No. It provides general nutrition and wellness decision support based on
available evidence. It does not diagnose, treat or prescribe.

### Can the pilot tell participants what they should eat?

It can organize package evidence and, where governed rules support it, explain
relevance to user-stated goals. It does not provide medical dietary treatment.

### Can a partner see individual scans or health records?

No. Pilot partners do not receive identifiable participant nutrition, scan,
diary, goal or Health Connect data.

### Can a food brand sponsor a favorable result?

No. Commercial participation cannot alter extraction, provenance or governed
interpretation.

### Is extraction always correct?

No. Image quality and label layout affect results. Uncertainty is shown, enhanced
processing can be slower, and users must verify the physical package—especially
for allergies.

### What does a pilot cost?

The discovery pack proposes no-cost or tightly bounded pilots. Any paid scope or
new infrastructure cost requires owner approval and a separate written proposal.

### What does the partner need to provide?

One coordinator, an approved invitation channel, a participant group, a suitable
venue/link for orientation and feedback on the agreed success measures.

### Can the partner use the NutriLens X logo?

Only after written approval for a specific asset and time period. A prospect
must not be announced as a partner before approval by both parties.

### Can results be published as research?

Not under the basic pilot. Research, publication or population analytics require
separate methodology, ethics/privacy/legal review and written approvals.

### What happens if the app is not release-ready for the pilot?

The pilot pauses. Outreach interest is not evidence that the product has passed
its technical or store release gates.
