# AI Architecture

The planned architecture is hybrid: computer vision and OCR produce structured label text; deterministic parsing and regulatory rules establish auditable facts; knowledge-graph and vector retrieval provide evidence; trained models personalize rankings; explainability exposes confidence, evidence and major factors; an optional local LLM verbalizes grounded results.

R0 contains only interfaces, folders and clearly labelled demonstrations. It contains no trained food detector, OCR engine, production recommender, RAG corpus, or validated clinical model.
